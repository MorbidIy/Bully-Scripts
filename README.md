## ⚠️ Installation Instructions: 
	Extract the contents within, or drag and drog the folder:

	1. Copy & Paste the folder to
	[Rockstar Games > Bully Scholarship Edition > _derpy_script_loader > scripts]
	2. Derpy's Script Loader will handle everything else in game.


## 💰 Player Status & Cheat Commands
	/immortal            -- Toggle ON/OFF to make Jimmy invulnerable to taking damage
	/innocent            -- Toggle ON/OFF to lock Jimmy's punishment points at zero
	/ammo                -- Toggle ON/OFF to give Jimmy infinite ammo
	/heal                -- Heals Jimmy to full health capacity
	/getmoney            -- Adds $1000 to Jimmy's wallet
	/getweapons          -- Adds 11 weapons to your inventory
	/getclothes          -- Unlockable Outfits get added to wardrobe
	/lethalshot          -- Weapon Damage Multiplied by 5000 (not fists)


## 🚗 Vehicle Spawning Commands
	/bmx                 -- Spawns Blue BMX bike
	/gokart              -- Spawns Black GoKart
	/scooter             -- Spawns Black Moped Scooter


## 🎱 Quick Teleportation Commands
	/dorm                -- Inside of Jimmy's room in the Boys' Dorm
	/school              -- The entrance to the Bullworths Academy hallways
	/lighthouse          -- The Harrington Lighthouse for the Prep
	/dragonswing         -- The Comic Book Store for the nerds
	/clubhouse           -- The Weight Lifting Room for the Jocks
	/blueballs           -- The BlueBalls' Billiard for the Greasers
	/townies             -- The Blue Skies Industrial Warehouse for Townies


## 🚬 For Shits & Giggles
	/kickme              -- Gives Jimmy the "Kick Me" sign
	/smoke               -- Smoke to regenerate health, but don't get busted
	/pack                -- Spawn three Pitbull's' to bodyguard Jimmy and attack those who hit you
